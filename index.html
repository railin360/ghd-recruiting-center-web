GOOD TO KNOW / 03

Questions?
Covered.

A few practical answers before you get started.

Where are these positions located?+

The recruiting location is Hopewell, Virginia. Specific delivery areas and assignments are discussed during screening.

How much does package delivery pay?+

The stated regular daily rate is $185 per day, with training at $17 per hour. Confirm the current compensation terms with the hiring team.

What's required during Peak?+

Package delivery applicants must be available six days per week during the Peak season.

Will I be employed directly by FedEx?+

No. GHD Logistics is a delivery contractor. This recruiting center is not a direct employment application to FedEx.

What happens after I apply?+

The hiring team reviews your application. If selected to advance, you'll receive additional screening instructions, including a potential background check and drug test.
